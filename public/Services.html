Technology Audit Services

An Information Technology audit examines and evaluates an organization's IT infrastructure, applications, data use and management, policies, procedures, and operational processes against recognized standards or established policies. At Sysware, we understand the need for occasional audits and provide:

01. IT Risk Assessment

Management requests to monitor and report on their risk posture are increasing. Common questions include:

  • Are we at risk?
  • How mature is our risk strategy?
  • How do we compare to peers from a benchmarking perspective?
  • Are we compliant with laws and regulations?
  • Are we prepared to comply with upcoming laws and regulations?
  • What is our strategy moving forward? Is our IT strategy aligned with our business and IT risks?

Our IT Audit practice provides capabilities and subject matter expertise to help clients understand areas of business and industry risk (governance, process, operations, and IT). We align IT risk components with the business and go beyond standard IT controls to ensure business-IT alignment.

02. Pre/Post Implementation Reviews

Detailed discussion between implementation team and the client to capture deliverables and expected timelines. Each step is documented for greater clarity and validation at both ends.

All key parameters are checked for necessary compliance once the system is implemented and ready to go live. Regular reviews are scheduled post-implementation to assess system performance and client satisfaction.

03. IT Internal Audit

Internal audits are conducted to check compliance of service delivery standards at various stages. This includes service tickets opened, escalations, response and resolution time.

04. Security Audit

Regular security audits are undertaken to assess the security of systems in place to handle customer information and system performance.
